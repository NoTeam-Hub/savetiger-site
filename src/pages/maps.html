Why there are no footprint maps

Tiger pugmarks and camera-trap locations are real, but publishing exact positions would hand poachers a map. Official reports share counts by state and landscape for the same reason, and so do we. If you see signs of poaching, call your forest department or the authorities listed under Take action.
