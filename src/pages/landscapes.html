India's tiger landscapes

Tigers do not stop at state borders, so the national census groups them into five landscapes. Central India now holds the most, and every landscape has grown since 2006. Select a landscape to highlight it.

Landscape totals are the official 2022 estimates (bars show 2006, 2010, 2014, 2018 and 2022; the Sundarbans were first reported in 2010). The census assigns whole states to a landscape, so state shading is a simplification: real landscapes cross state lines, and northern West Bengal is counted with the North-East. Source: Press Information Bureau, Annexure I. The published North-East subtotal (236) is a little below the sum of its state rows (240); we show the published figure.
